New Student Registration

Fill in your details to apply for admission. All fields marked * are mandatory.

  1. Personal details – your full name, roll number and a valid e-mail address.
  2. Course – choose the programme you are applying for.
  3. Password – at least 8 characters with upper-case, lower-case, a number and a special character.
  4. Documents – a profile photo (JPG, PNG or WEBP, max 2 MB) and optionally an ID proof (PDF, JPG or PNG, max 5 MB).
Every field is checked as you type. The form cannot be submitted until all errors are fixed.
